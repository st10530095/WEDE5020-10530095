Welcome to Golden Crust Bakery

Since 2015, Golden Crust Bakery has been baking fresh bread, custom cakes, and pastries for the local Durban community. What started as a small home-baking operation has grown into a beloved neighbourhood bakery, built on traditional techniques and locally sourced ingredients.

What We Offer

Artisanal Bread

Sourdough, rye, and whole-wheat loaves baked fresh every morning.

Custom Cakes

Birthday, wedding, and celebration cakes designed to order.

Pastries

Croissants, muffins, and sweet treats baked daily.
