Brief History
Golden Crust Bakery is a family-owned bakery based in Newlands West, Durban, established in 2015. It began as a small home-baking operation run out of a family kitchen and has since grown into a beloved neighbourhood bakery known for artisanal bread, custom cakes, and pastries. Over the past decade, the bakery has built a loyal local customer base primarily through word-of-mouth referrals, community events, and school fundraisers, and has become a recognisable name for freshly baked goods in the surrounding suburbs of Durban.
Mission and Vision
Mission: To bring high-quality, freshly baked goods to the community using traditional baking techniques and locally sourced ingredients, while offering friendly, personal service that reflects the bakery's family roots.
Vision: To become the go-to bakery for celebrations and everyday treats across Durban, known for consistency, warmth, and craftsmanship, and to eventually expand into a second branch within the greater eThekwini area.
Target Audience
Local families and young professionals in Newlands West and surrounding Durban suburbs seeking quality baked goods, as well as customers planning events such as birthdays, weddings, and corporate functions who require custom cakes and catering. A secondary audience includes schools and community organisations seeking bulk orders for fundraisers and events.
Proposed Website Features and Functionality: The website will feature a Homepage with a hero image and introduction, an About Us page detailing the bakery's history, a Products page showcasing bread, cakes, and pastries with pricing, an Enquiry page for custom cake orders, and a Contact page listing two locations with a map and contact form.
